About Me

I’m Wolverine — here to explore my creativity and put my imagination on display. Ever since I was young, I’ve always wanted to create an interactive fantasy world of my own. A place where my imagination could exist, move, and feel real. And somehow, through coding and building this website, that dream actually became possible. In my mind, I always imagined creating a unique and wonderful version of myself — an imaginary profile that feels alive. That’s exactly why this website is called My Profile. It’s not just a profile… it’s a piece of my imagination brought to life. I was deeply inspired by a creator whose way of presenting her profile was incredibly creative and beautiful. Seeing her work made me want to create something of my own, something personal, something meaningful. That’s where this journey truly began. With the help of modern tools like GitHub, ChatGPT, Gemini, and many other platforms, I was able to turn my ideas into reality. I won’t say that I’m some big coder who built everything alone — that wouldn’t be true. I still only know the basics. But I will take credit for the idea, the vision, and the effort behind it. This was my original imagination, my original concept, and I brought it to life in my own way. And honestly… the most fun part wasn’t even the final result. It was the process. The coding. The experimenting. Writing prompts. Shaping my imagination into something interactive and real. This is my first time doing something like this, so of course it took a lot of time. Every new feature came with errors. Fixing them took even more time. Sometimes it was frustrating, sometimes it felt like pure brain rot. But in the end… it was all worth it. Because when something finally worked — when it looked even close to what I imagined — that feeling was unmatched. Maybe it’s not always perfectly perfect… but it’s mine. And that’s what makes it special. Thanks for visiting my little art piece~ 🎀
